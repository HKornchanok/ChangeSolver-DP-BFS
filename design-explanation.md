## Desktop
![[Desktop][1]Home - full function.png](design%2FDesktop%2F%5BDesktop%5D%5B1%5DHome%20-%20full%20function.png)

The page have two main part, **Product List** and **Current Order**

### Product List
- Listing product from left to right, then up to down.
- When the products overflowed the screen, user can scroll through all the products vertically while the Search box and the Title section are still visible.
- Search box for searching a product by name.
- Selecting a product by clicking on the cart icon on the product card, adjusting the quantity by clicking on the plus and minus icon.

### Current Order
- Display all the selected products.
- Adjusting the quantity of the selected products.
- Showing the combination of coins, based on the total price of the selected products and 
the total amount of money given by the customer, to be given back to the customer as change.
  - _NOTE: While the design shows multiple combination of coins. You only need to display one, if you do not wish to do the bonus task._

## Mobile

The Mobile version is very much the same as the Desktop version just with the
**Current Order** section moved to the bottom instead of being on the side.
